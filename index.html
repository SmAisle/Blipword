How to Play

Every day there are 10 words. Each one flashes on the screen for a split second, then disappears.

  • Tap Flash when you're ready. Blocks show where the word will be, the word blips on, then the blocks cover it again.
  • Pick the definition of the word you saw from 4 choices.
  • The other 3 choices belong to look-alike words (BRAKE, BREAK, BRAVE, BAKER), so read carefully.
  • Each word flashes once, and each one goes a little faster than the last. Longer words stay up longer, so every word is fair: a short word on word 1 shows for about a third of a second.
  • The last 3 words are Hard: long, rare words. Get all 10 for a perfect day.
  • Pick a speed before you start: 🐢 Slow, ⚡ Normal or 🔥 Fast. Aced Normal? Try Fast. Your speed is locked in once the first word flashes.

A new game comes out every day at midnight Eastern time. Past days are in the Archive. Want to warm up first? Try Practice in the menu.

By playing, you agree to the smAisle Terms and Privacy. The game counts opens and plays anonymously to see how many people play. No names, no accounts.
